What you are approving

The pass deposit address, token, amount, and pool fee are public. The wallet receiving the resulting pass is handled as a private STRK20 note. This pass is a bearer permission: it can only trigger the fixed payment rule; it cannot withdraw your wallet balance or change the recipient.
