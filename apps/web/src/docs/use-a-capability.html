Use a pass.
Not a master key.

A pass can do only the job written into its public policy. The app shows that job in plain language; your privacy wallet handles the private work.

01

Connect

Connect a compatible Starknet privacy wallet on Mainnet.

02

Choose

Select the pass and review its permitted action, cap, recipient, and expiry.

03

Confirm

Review and confirm in your wallet. The wallet owns proof construction and submission.

Privacy boundary

The pass holder is not placed in the Gatekeeper call. The target action, timing, and state change remain public. Relay behavior is required for transaction-sender separation.
