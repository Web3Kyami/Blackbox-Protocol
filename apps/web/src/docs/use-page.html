DOCS / 03 / HOLDER FLOW

Use a pass.
Not a master key.

The holder sees one plain-language job. The wallet owns the private notes and proof construction; the contract enforces the public boundaries.

HOLDER SEQUENCE

Connect, review, confirm, verify.

BlackBox does not ask the holder for a private key, viewing key, or note plaintext.

01

Receive

The issuer privately delivers one bounded pass to the chosen holder wallet.

02

Connect

The holder connects that wallet on Starknet Mainnet.

03

Review

The app shows the fixed job; the wallet shows the proof-backed transaction.

04

Confirm

After approval, Mainnet receipt and public policy state confirm the result.

OPEN THE HOLDER APP

See what your wallet can use.

After connection, the app shows the available permission, No pass available, or the completed payment state. It never invents a private-note inventory.
